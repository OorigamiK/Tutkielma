		In this paper we extend the paper of O'Sullivan et al. on interval sums of prime powers to a class of regularly varying functions with index $\alpha>1$, under mild additional assumptions. Let $n_f(x)$ be the length of the largest interval with sum less than $x$ and $S_f(x)$ denote the number of interval sums less than $x$. Our main theorem states that $S_f(x)\sim n_f(x)^2C(\alpha)$, where $C(\alpha)$ is constant dependent only on $\alpha$. This paper applies tools from the theory of regularly varying functions and relates the number of blocks of length $i$ to $\phi(x)=(1+x^{\alpha+1})^{\frac{1}{\alpha+1}}-x$.
